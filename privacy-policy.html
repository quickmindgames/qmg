Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Quick Mind Games ("we", "our", or "us") develops and publishes mobile puzzle, logic, brain-training, and casual games.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use our games and services.

Information We Collect

Information You Provide

Most of our games do not require account creation or registration. We do not intentionally collect personal information such as:

  • Name

  • Email address

  • Phone number

  • Postal address

unless you voluntarily provide such information when contacting us for support.

Automatically Collected Information

To improve the quality, performance, and stability of our games, certain information may be collected automatically, including:

  • Device model

  • Operating system version

  • App version

  • Language settings

  • Crash reports

  • Diagnostic information

  • Gameplay and usage statistics

This information is generally collected in an aggregated or anonymized form and is used to improve the user experience.

Analytics and Performance Monitoring

Our games may use third-party analytics and performance monitoring services, including:

  • Firebase Analytics

  • Firebase Crashlytics

  • Firebase Remote Config

These services help us:

  • Understand how players use our games

  • Improve gameplay and user experience

  • Identify and fix crashes or technical issues

  • Deliver configuration updates and feature improvements

These services may collect device and usage information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For more information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

Advertising

Some current or future games published by Quick Mind Games may display advertisements or use advertising-related services.

Where applicable, advertising technologies may collect identifiers and usage information to provide and measure advertisements.

If advertising is introduced into a game, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect the relevant practices.

Local Data Storage

Our games may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Game progress

  • High scores

  • Statistics

  • Achievements

  • Settings and preferences

  • Daily streaks and gameplay history

This information is stored primarily to provide core game functionality and improve the user experience.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers only when necessary to:

  • Operate our games

  • Analyze performance

  • Diagnose technical issues

  • Comply with legal obligations

Children's Privacy

Our games are intended for a general audience.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through one of our games, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through our games. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Services

Our games may use third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Google Play Services

  • Firebase Analytics

  • Firebase Crashlytics

  • Firebase Remote Config

These services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
